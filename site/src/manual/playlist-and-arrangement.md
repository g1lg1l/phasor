# Playlist and arrangement

The song: clips of patterns, sounds and automation on lanes, from left to right.

## 1. Place clips

Right-click a lane to insert a pattern, or drag one from the browser. The tools on top: select, draw, paint copies, erase, slice, mute. Ctrl+D duplicates forward.

## 2. Lanes

Click a lane to fold it, double-click to rename; M and S mute and solo it. Right-click for color, insert and delete.

## 3. Loop and markers

Right-drag along the ruler to loop a region. Markers sit on the ruler: click one to jump there, drag it, double-click to rename it.

## 4. Audio clips

Double-click one for its properties, laid out like a sampler's: its sound on top (the handles, or Start and End, cut it; the envelope picked under it is drawn over it), One-shot or Loop, pitch, gain, fades, how it follows the tempo (Off, Resample, Stretch), and MIX: the track it plays into, and an EQ, a reverb, a delay and any effect for this sound alone (the first touch gives it a mixer track of its own). Right-click: split it into drums and tones, or turn a melody into notes.

## 5. A song from loops

Create → Song from the patterns lays your patterns out as intro, build, drop, break, drop 2 and outro, with markers. Then change what you like.
