# Mixer and effects

Every channel plays into a mixer track; tracks feed buses and the master.

## 1. Tracks

Mixer (F9): a strip per track with pan, a three-band EQ (low, mid, high), fader and meter; M silences one, S solos it with whatever it feeds and whatever feeds it. + Track adds one.

## 2. Routing

Pull a cable from a strip's jack onto another: that track plays into it (a bus). With Shift, a send instead, for a shared reverb or delay.

## 3. Effects

Click a strip: its effects open on the right, top to bottom before the fader. + Effect adds an EQ Three, Compressor, Filter, Saturator, Chorus, Delay, Reverb, Limiter or a plugin.

## 4. Ducking

Duck to… makes the track dip whenever another one plays: the bass under the kick, in one click.

## 5. Macros and LFOs

Right-click any knob or fader to map a macro or an LFO onto it, or to MIDI-learn it; the amounts are on Modulation (F10).
