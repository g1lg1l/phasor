# Make it yours

Looks, keys and layouts: all in Preferences (Ctrl ,) and the View menu.

## 1. Themes

Preferences → Look: pick a theme, or Edit theme… to change any color, live, and export it to share it; Import… brings one in. Reduce motion turns the animations off.

## 2. Keys

Preferences → Keys: presets like FL Studio's and Ableton's, or click any shortcut to change it. F1 lists them all.

## 3. Layout

Split an editor in two, or Shift with an editor's key (F6, F7, F9, F10) to open it in its own window. Workspaces keep a whole layout: make a new one from the current layout and switch between them.

## 4. Language

Ravune speaks English and Italian: switch in Preferences → Look.
