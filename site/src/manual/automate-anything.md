# Automate anything

Make a knob move over time: on the playlist, inside a pattern, or on one clip.

## 1. On the playlist

Right-click any knob → Create automation clip: a line under everything, as long as the song. Click it to add points, drag them, right-click one to delete it.

## 2. Inside a pattern

Right-click a knob → Automate in "pattern": the line lives in the pattern and plays wherever the pattern does. Draw it under the piano roll, in AUTO.

## 3. On a sound's clip

Double-click an audio clip: its Envelopes move its volume, pan and a DJ filter. You can also edit those lines right on the playlist.

## 4. Modulation and controllers

Right-click a knob → a macro or an LFO (their amounts are on Modulation, F10), or MIDI learn to move it from a controller.
