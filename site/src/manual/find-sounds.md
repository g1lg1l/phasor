# Find sounds

The browser on the left: the built-in kit and genre packs, your folders, free sounds online.

## 1. Listen

Sounds: click a sound or arrow onto it to hear it (▶ always plays it). The kit has drums, percussion, 808s, FX and packs for House, Techno, Hardgroove, Drum & Bass, Liquid DnB, IDM and Electronic.

## 2. Add it

Double-click or Enter adds it as a sampler channel; or drag it onto the rack, or onto the playlist as an audio clip. ☆ keeps it in Favorites.

## 3. Your own folders

Add a folder with your sounds… (or drop a folder onto the window). The search box filters the kit, favorites and every folder, subfolders too.

## 4. Free sounds online

Online: Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, ccMixter, Freesound (free key) or a source of your own. Only free licenses; what you use is kept with its license, and File → Export credits… writes them down.

## 5. Instruments and plugins

Synths holds VA-1 presets; .sfz and .dspreset instruments sit next to your samples. Plugins lists the CLAP and VST3 plugins it found; each runs in its own process.
