# Your first beat

From an empty project to a beat you can save, in six steps.

## 1. Pick sounds

In the browser on the left, Sounds: click a sound to hear it, double-click it (or Enter) to add it as a channel. The kit's Kicks, Snares and Hi-hats are a good start.

## 2. Paint steps

Channel Rack (F6): click the squares to place hits, drag across to paint, hold the right button to erase. Each row is a channel; 16 steps make a bar.

## 3. Play it

Space plays and stops. PAT loops the pattern you're on, SONG plays the playlist.

## 4. Add a melody

Synths in the browser: add a VA-1 preset. Then the Piano Roll (F7): click to draw notes, drag to move them, drag the right edge to resize. The lane underneath sets each note's velocity.

## 5. Arrange

Right-click the playlist to insert the pattern, or drag it in from Patterns in the browser. Select clips and Ctrl+D to keep duplicating forward.

## 6. Mix and save

Mixer (F9): faders, pan, and each track's effects on the right. Ctrl+S saves the project; File → Export renders it as WAV, FLAC, Ogg or MP3.
