# Channel Rack and patterns

Patterns are short loops: each channel has a row of steps, and its notes in the piano roll.

## 1. Channels

Each row is a channel: a sampler, a VA-1 or a plugin. M mutes it, S solos it; drop another sound or a plugin on its row to replace what it plays.

## 2. Steps

Click to toggle, drag to paint, hold the right button to erase. Each step is a 16th; louder and softer hits are the velocity lane in the piano roll.

## 3. Length and swing

The pattern's length is in 16ths: drag it, type any length, right-click for bars. Swing pushes every second 16th late for a shuffle.

## 4. More patterns

F4 makes a new pattern; all of them are in the browser's Patterns tab, where you rename, color and drag them onto the playlist.

## 5. Channel settings

Select a channel: its settings open on the right of the rack and the piano roll (F8 shows or hides them). The sampler's knobs, the VA-1's, or the plugin; the mixer track it plays into and that track's effects.
