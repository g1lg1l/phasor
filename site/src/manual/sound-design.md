# Sound design

Shape what a channel sounds like: the VA-1, the sampler, instruments and plugins.

## 1. VA-1

The built-in synth: two oscillators (sine, triangle, saw, square; the second one tuned and detuned), a low-pass filter with resonance and its own envelope, and the amp envelope. Synths in the browser has presets; the dice rolls a new patch in musical ranges.

## 2. Sampler

A sound in the rack. Its panel is laid out like an audio clip's: the sound on top (drag its handles, or turn Start and End, to cut it; press it to hear it), One-shot or Loop (round and round while the note is held), Pitch, Volume, then Attack, Release and Choke (open and closed hats in one group cut each other), then MIX: the track it plays into, and an EQ, a reverb, a delay and any effect for this sound alone (the first touch gives it a mixer track of its own).

## 3. Instruments

.sfz and .dspreset multi-sample instruments play across the keys, with round robins, loops and release, like a sampled piano.

## 4. Plugins

CLAP and VST3 instruments and effects from the browser's Plugins tab, or dropped on a channel to replace what it plays. Each runs in its own process: if one crashes it goes quiet, Ravune carries on, and Reload on its device brings it back.

## 5. Groove

Right-click a channel → Swing to give it a swing of its own: straight hats over a shuffled beat, or the other way round.
