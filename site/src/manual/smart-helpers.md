# Smart helpers

Small tools that listen to your sounds, all offline.

## 1. Drums and tones

Right-click an audio clip → Split into drums and tones: two new clips under it, the hits and everything that holds a pitch, with the original muted.

## 2. Notes from a sound

Right-click a clip with one voice (a bassline, a vocal, a lead) → Notes from this sound: its notes in a new pattern for a VA-1.

## 3. Tempo of a loop

Ravune reads a loop's tempo from its name or its beat, so it follows the song: Stretch keeps the pitch, Resample plays it like a turntable.
