# Modulation

Knobs that move by themselves: macros, LFOs, recorded moves and controllers.

## 1. Macros

Eight knobs on Modulation (F10), each moving whatever you map to it: right-click any knob → a macro, or Alt+drag a macro onto the knob.

## 2. Rand and variations

Rand throws every mapped macro somewhere new; + keeps where they are as a variation, and a click on its number brings them back.

## 3. LFOs

+ LFO adds one: sine, triangle, saw, square or random (a new level each cycle), synced to the bar or free in Hz. Drag its handle onto any knob to map it.

## 4. Amounts

Every mapping is listed under the sources with its amount, a share of the knob's whole travel; a negative amount turns it upside down.

## 5. Record the moves

Arm the automation button next to Record and turn knobs while the song plays: the moves land in automation clips, one undo step per turn.

## 6. Controllers

Right-click → MIDI learn, then move a knob on your controller. MPE controllers bend, press and slide every note on its own.
