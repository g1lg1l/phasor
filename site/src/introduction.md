# Ravune

A modular DAW for deep sound design. Approachable on day one, deep enough for a lifetime.
Free and open source, forever: no tiers, no track limits. AI stays optional, local-first
and off by default.

![Ravune: the playlist, the mixer and the browser](/images/ravune.png)

## What's inside

- **Make**: a channel rack and a piano roll, two views of the same notes; chords, per-note
  pitch and brightness, knob automation inside patterns, MPE. A sampler with attack and
  release, SFZ and Decent Sampler instruments, the VA-1 synth.
- **Arrange**: an FL-style playlist where any clip goes on any lane, automation clips from
  any knob (drawn or recorded), clip envelopes, loops that follow the tempo, markers and a
  song built from your patterns.
- **Mix**: a mixer with sends, buses and cables, 8 built-in effects, one-click sidechain,
  macros with Rand and variations, LFOs, MIDI learn.
- **Plug in**: CLAP and VST3, each in its own process. A crashing plugin goes quiet,
  Ravune carries on.
- **Find**: a CC0 library with packs for House, Techno, Hardgroove, Drum & Bass, Liquid
  DnB, IDM and Electronic, your own folders, and free sounds online with their credits.
- **Start fast**: seven genre templates, interactive lessons, this manual inside the app
  (Help → Manual), DAWproject to and from other DAWs.
- **Script it, talk to it**: Luau scripts and an MCP server, so Claude or any agent can
  build, listen and undo in one step.
- **Make it yours**: themes, keymaps (FL Studio and Ableton layouts too), workspaces,
  extensions, English and Italian.
