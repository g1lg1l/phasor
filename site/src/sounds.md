# Sound library

Everything built in is CC0: use it in anything, commercial or not, no credit needed.

- **The kit**: kicks, snares, claps, hi-hats, cymbals, toms, percussion, 808s and FX, plus
  vocal chops. The sounds at the top level were synthesized for Ravune; the rest are CC0
  recordings from Freesound and the Versilian Community Sample Library.
- **Genre packs**, loops cut to whole bars at the tempo in their name:

| Pack | Sounds | What's in it |
|---|---|---|
| House | 27 | deep and punchy kicks, claps, hats, shaker, ride, funky drum loops, bongos and congas, chord stabs and loops, organ, basslines, a vocal chop loop |
| Techno | 16 | tight, subby and distorted kicks, rumble, hats and hat loops, percussion loops, stabs |
| Hardgroove | 15 | bongo, conga and percussion loops at 140 to 145, toms, rims, metal, cowbell, a shout |
| Drum & Bass | 14 | breaks at 174 and 175, punchy kick, snares, reese basses, a neuro stab |
| Liquid DnB | 14 | liquid breaks, Rhodes and e-piano chords, pads, a sub, soft snare and rim |
| IDM | 18 | glitches, clicks, FM blips, crushed drums, granular textures, odd-meter loops |
| Electronic | 34 | leads, arps, pads, risers and downlifters, impacts, zaps, synth and 808 basses, chords, plucks |

The House and Electronic packs are stereo where the recording is. Every recording's
author and source are credited in
[assets/library/LICENSE.txt](https://github.com/g1lg1l/ravune/blob/main/assets/library/LICENSE.txt).

## More sounds

The browser's **Online** tab searches Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, ccMixter, Freesound
(with a free key) or a source of your own. Only free licenses: CC0 and CC BY by default,
share-alike and non-commercial when you ask. A sound you use is kept with its license, and
File → Export credits… writes them all down for your release notes.
