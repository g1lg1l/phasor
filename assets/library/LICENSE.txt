The sounds at the top of this folder were synthesized from scratch by
crates/ravune-engine/examples/make_kit.rs: no recordings, no third-party samples. The Ravune
contributors waive all copyright and related rights to them (CC0).

The sounds in the subfolders are recordings their authors released under CC0, from Freesound
(found through Openverse; the HQ previews) and the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL).
They were converted to 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV, trimmed and peak-normalized. The genre packs are
FLAC instead (Drum & Bass, Liquid DnB, Techno, Hardgroove and IDM in mono; House and Electronic
in stereo where the recording is), their loops cut to whole bars at the tempo in their name.
